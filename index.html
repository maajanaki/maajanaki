Pavitra Tirth Sthal

Explore the 5 most sacred sites near Maa Janki Stay — all within the holy land of Sitamarhi, the birthplace of Maa Sita.

Most Sacred
🛕
Punaura Dham
पुनौरा धाम · 4 km from Stay
The most revered site — believed to be the exact birthplace of Maa Sita (Janaki). A grand temple complex with sacred pond, daily aarti and Ram-Janaki vivahotsav celebrations.
Main Mandir
🙏
Janki Mandir
जानकी मंदिर · 2 km
Ancient temple dedicated to Maa Janaki — centre of daily puja, bhajan-kirtan, and Navratri mahotsav.
🔱
Haleshwar Sthan
हलेश्वर स्थान · 5 km
Sacred Shiv temple with ancient linga. Devotees from across Bihar come for Mahashivratri and Monday worship.
💧
Pant Pakhar
पंत पखार · 3 km
A sacred pond believed to be used by Maa Sita. Peaceful surroundings, ideal for morning meditation and holy dip.
🛤️
Janakpur Road
जनकपुर मार्ग · Nepal Border
The historic route connecting Sitamarhi to Janakpur (Nepal) — birthplace of King Janak. Lined with small mandirs and ashrams.
